# Sec 2 华文 (Chinese MT) — EOY Revision Guide

---

## 0. 考试结构 (Exam Format Overview)

Typical Sec 2 CL paper components:

| Component | 考什么 (Tests) |
|---|---|
| 听力 (Listening) | Understanding spoken Mandarin, answering questions based on audio |
| 口试 (Oral) | 看图说话 (picture-based conversation) + 对话 (conversation with examiner) |
| 写作 (Composition) | 记叙文 (narrative) usually, based on a topic or picture prompt |
| 语文运用 (Language Use) | 词语/句子填空 (vocab/sentence fill-in-blanks), 语法 (grammar), 改错 (error correction) |
| 阅读理解 (Comprehension) | 短文/语段理解, answering questions based on a passage |

---

## 1. 写作 (COMPOSITION) — Biggest Weightage, Most Improvable

**Structure that scores well (记叙文 narrative structure):**
1. **开头 (Opening)** — set the scene, introduce time/place/characters, hook the reader
2. **发展 (Development)** — build up events, include 对话 (dialogue) and 心理描写 (inner thoughts/feelings) to add depth
3. **高潮 (Climax)** — the turning point / most dramatic moment
4. **结尾 (Ending)** — resolve the story AND include a reflection/lesson learnt (感想) — this reflection at the end is often specifically rewarded in the marking scheme, don't skip it

**Common mark-losers:**
- 文不对题 (writing doesn't match the topic) — always re-read the topic/prompt before finishing, make sure your story actually addresses it
- 平铺直叙 (flat, event-only storytelling with no emotion/description) — examiners want 细节描写 (descriptive detail) not just "然后...然后...然后"
- Overusing simple/repetitive connectors like "然后" (then) — instead use varied transition words: 于是、接着、没想到、突然、与此同时

**High-value phrases/structures to have ready (成语 & 句型):**
- Emotion/description 成语: 兴高采烈 (elated), 垂头丧气 (dejected), 恍然大悟 (suddenly realized), 无地自容 (extremely embarrassed)
- Useful sentence openers: 记得那是一个……的日子 (I remember it was a... day), 事情是这样的 (this is how it happened)

## 2. 语文运用 (LANGUAGE USE) — Vocab, Grammar, Error Correction

**Common tested grammar points at this level:**
- 量词 (measure words) — matching the correct measure word to the noun (一**本**书, 一**条**路, 一**位**老师) — this is one of the most-tested and most-forgotten areas since English has no equivalent
- 关联词 (connective pairs) — must be used correctly as PAIRS: 虽然...但是..., 因为...所以..., 不但...而且..., 一...就...
- 近义词辨析 (synonym discrimination) — choosing the correct word among similar-meaning words based on context/register (formal vs informal, degree of intensity)

**改错 (Error correction) technique:** read the sentence slowly, checking for: wrong measure word, missing/wrong connective, wrong word order (Chinese word order rules differ from English — time/place usually comes BEFORE the verb, unlike English), incorrect character usage for near-homophones

---

## 3. 阅读理解 (COMPREHENSION)

- Read the question FIRST before the passage, so you know what to look for
- Answers must be based on the passage — don't add outside knowledge unless the question specifically asks you to infer/give your own opinion
- For 归纳/概括 (summarising) type questions, extract the key idea in your OWN words (not copying full sentences) — same principle as English summary

---

## 4. 口试 (ORAL)

**看图说话 (Picture description):**
- Don't just list what's in the picture — build a mini-narrative: what's happening, why, what might happen next, and how the people might be feeling
- Use varied sentence structures and some 成语 if natural — but don't force complex vocabulary awkwardly, fluency and clarity matter more than showing off

**对话 (Conversation):**
- Answer in FULL sentences, not one-word answers — examiners assess your ability to express and elaborate, not just comprehension
- If you don't understand a question, it's fine to ask for clarification politely in Chinese rather than staying silent
